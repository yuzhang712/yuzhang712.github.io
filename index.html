Biography


I graduated with a Ph.D. in Computer Science and Engineering from The Chinese University of Hong Kong (CUHK) in July 2026, advised by Prof. Bei Yu. I also worked as a research intern at NVIDIA Research, focusing on LLM/VLM hallucination detection. My research interests are efficient and reliable LLM training/inference, agentic EDA, and software/hardware co-design. Besides research, I love swimming and hiking in my spare time.
